What the ratio means

Overall per $1 is the overall score divided by estimated spend. It is a derived ratio, not an official rank. It rewards cheap models, is undefined for free models, and says nothing about whether a score is useful for your analysis. Compare free models on score alone; compare paid models on both.

The spend figure is a token-price estimate, not an invoice. Cached input, reasoning tokens and provider discounts are priced by the table in harness/task_usage.py; actual Go billing is account-wide. Task scores are not a coding-difficulty ranking, so a low-cost model is not automatically the better engineering choice.
