Data dictionary

Fields in results.jsonl.
FieldMeaning
edition_idEdition the row belongs to. Never compare across editions.
model_slugStable model identifier used in URLs and joins.
task_idTask identifier, for example QTL-01.
sectionBenchmark section; joins to sections.json.
scoreWeighted task score in [0, 1]. A timeout or error may be zero or a partial score; read the status field.
passWhether the score met the task threshold.
statuscompleted, timeout, error or budget.
runtime_secTask runtime in seconds, as recorded by the harness.
sub_scoresOrdered named sub-scores, in the same order as the task weights.

Section and overall scores are not stored; they are computed from these rows by the site build and checked against the harness output. This makes the public data smaller and removes a source of disagreement.

What is withheld

The export is an allowlist. It excludes prompts, task inputs, mounted paths, grader and baseline code, truth tables, truth-side diagnostics, agent transcripts, work directories and absolute filesystem paths. PROVENANCE.json records the exported field list, the omitted material and a SHA-256 for each source artifact.
