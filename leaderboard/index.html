How to read this table

Aggregation

A task score is a weighted sum of its sub-scores. A section score is the mean of its tasks. The overall score is the macro-average of section scores, so a section with more tasks does not dominate. Errors and timeouts still contribute zero, exactly as in the harness.

Status is separate from score

A timeout is displayed as a timeout, not as an ordinary zero. Its row keeps any partial score the harness could grade, so latency still affects the aggregate without hiding why the run ended. The distinction matters when comparing capability with latency.

Edition policy

Models are only compared within an edition: same dataset, benchmark version and protocol. New protocols create a new edition; old editions stay archived. Do not compare scores across editions.

Coverage

26 tasks are planned for the benchmark. This edition runs the implemented subset, so the overall score describes that subset. Missing tasks are not imputed.
